Cloud Cost Trends - Q3 2026 Summary

Enterprise cloud spend continued its gradual shift from on-demand compute toward committed-use and savings-plan commitments through the third quarter of 2026. Across the organizations surveyed, average monthly spend rose about 6% quarter over quarter, driven mainly by GPU and inference workloads rather than general-purpose compute.

Key points

  • Inference and GPU compute were the largest single contributor to quarter-over-quarter growth.
  • Committed-use discounts offset roughly a third of the on-demand increase.
  • Storage and egress remained flat as a share of total spend.
